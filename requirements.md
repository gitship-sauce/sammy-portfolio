# My portfolio: requirements

A website about me and my projects, for software internship applications.

## Anyone can

- See who I am, in one line, and my projects as cards.
- Open a project and see what it does, what I did on it, a picture, and a live link.
- Find my email and GitHub.

## Only me

- Log in with my username and password, on an admin page.
- Add, edit and delete projects, and upload a picture for each.
- Change the content without editing code, like a C M S.

## Tools

- Next.js, live on Vercel.
- Projects and pictures saved in a Neon database.
- All on free plans.

## Not now

- Blog, dark mode, comments, visitor stats.
